{{< intro >}}
New York Kayak Polo is a 100% volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization devoted to developing and promoting the sport of kayak polo in the New York City area. Everybody is welcome to come and learn — your first session is free!
{{< /intro >}}

## Who we are

We are a community of paddlers of all ages, abilities, and experience levels who share a love of kayak polo. We run introductory, new-player, and regular sessions year-round — outdoors on the Hudson River in the summer and in an indoor pool in the winter — and our members compete in tournaments across North America and beyond.

## How the club works

New York Kayak Polo is run **by its members**, for its members. As a nonprofit, we have no paid staff: everything from coaching and equipment maintenance to scheduling and event organizing is done by volunteers. Membership dues and donations cover our costs — pool and water access, boats and equipment, insurance, and travel support so that players of all budgets can take part.

Members elect a **Leadership Committee** each year to handle the club's day-to-day operations and governance. Members can vote in club elections, run for leadership positions, and help shape the direction of the club.
